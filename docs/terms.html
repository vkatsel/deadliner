2. Description of the Service

Deadliner is a client-side productivity tool designed to help university students aggregate coursework assignments and class schedules from learning management systems (e.g., Moodle, Google Classroom) and university scheduling portals (e.g., schedule.kse.ua), synchronizing them into Google Calendar.

Deadliner operates as a standalone local CLI. It does not provide server-side hosted services or continuous cloud infrastructure. All operations run strictly on your local computer.
