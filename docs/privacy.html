Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026 • Last Updated: September 23, 2026 • Version 2.1
Core Privacy Commitment: Deadliner is a local-first, zero-knowledge application. We do not operate an external database, remote server, or telemetry collector. Your academic credentials, tokens, and course data remain on your local computer at all times.

1. Overview & Architectural Model

Deadliner is an open-source command-line tool built by university students for university students to aggregate academic coursework and class schedules from Moodle, Google Classroom, and Kyiv School of Economics (KSE) Schedule into personal calendars.

Deadliner does not transmit personal data to third parties. All network communication occurs directly and securely between your local client machine and the official APIs of Moodle, Google, and schedule.kse.ua via TLS/HTTPS.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

When you run Deadliner, it accesses only the data strictly necessary to display or sync your assignments and classes:

  • Coursework & Deadlines: Assignment titles, due dates, course names, and submission URLs from Moodle and Google Classroom.
  • Class Schedule: Discipline titles, lecture/practice designations, scheduled time slots, and room numbers from schedule.kse.ua.
  • Calendar Events: Events created by Deadliner in your designated Google Calendar to add, update, or remove synchronized entries.

3. Google API Scopes & Limited Use Disclosure

Mandatory Google API Disclosure:
Deadliner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deadliner requests the following OAuth 2.0 scopes strictly to provide student calendar synchronization:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: Used solely to create, update, and delete academic deadline reminders and timetable entries on your primary Google Calendar (color-coded: Tomato Red for deadlines, Sage Green for lectures, Peacock Blue for practices). Deadliner tags its events with private metadata (deadliner_id) and never reads, modifies, or deletes calendar events that it did not create.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.courses.readonly: Used solely to list the active academic courses in which you are enrolled as a student.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.coursework.me.readonly & https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.coursework.students.readonly: Used solely to retrieve your student coursework assignments, submission statuses, and deadlines.

Limited Use Compliance Pillars

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements:

  • No External Transfer: Deadliner never transfers Google user data to third parties or remote servers. All API responses are processed in-memory and on your local disk.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used or transferred to serve advertisements, including personalized, contextual, or interest-based ads.
  • No AI/ML Training: Google user data is never used or transferred to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Human Access Restrictions: No human beings (including Deadliner developers or maintainers) ever read or inspect your Google user data. The software operates entirely autonomously on your local workstation.

4. Local Storage & Security

Authentication tokens (Google OAuth refresh tokens, Moodle tokens, and KSE schedule tokens) and OAuth client configurations are written strictly to your local machine under your user home directory:

  • ~/.deadliner.json — General configuration and platform tokens.
  • ~/.deadliner_google_token.json — Google OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • ~/.deadliner/client_secret.json — Google OAuth Desktop Client credentials.

These files are protected by your operating system's local user access permissions. Deadliner never uploads these credentials to any cloud server or repository.

5. Zero Third-Party Sharing & Telemetry

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data.
  • We do not include third-party tracking scripts, analytics cookies, or behavioral telemetry in our CLI or on this documentation website.
  • All network traffic is encrypted via standard HTTPS/TLS directly between your device and the respective official service providers (Google LLC, Moodle, KSE).

6. Data Retention and How to Revoke Access

Because Deadliner stores no data remotely, revoking access and removing all traces is instantaneous and completely under your control:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete all configuration files and cached tokens at any time: 
    # macOS & Linux:
rm -f ~/.deadliner.json ~/.deadliner_google_token.json ~/.deadliner/client_secret.json

# Windows (PowerShell):
Remove-Item "$HOME\.deadliner.json", "$HOME\.deadliner_google_token.json", "$HOME\.deadliner\client_secret.json" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
  • Google OAuth Revocation: Revoke authorization at any time through your Google Account Security Dashboard at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google will immediately invalidate all refresh tokens.
  • Calendar Event Removal: Disabling sync or deleting Deadliner events from Google Calendar permanently deletes them with no residual copies stored anywhere.
