Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026 • Last Updated: September 18, 2026 • Version 2.0
Summary of Core Commitment: Deadliner is a local-first, zero-knowledge application. We do not operate an external database, remote server, or telemetry collector. Your academic credentials and course data remain on your own computer at all times.

1. Overview & Architectural Model

Deadliner is an open-source command-line tool built by university students for university students to aggregate academic coursework and class schedules from Moodle, Google Classroom, and Kyiv School of Economics (KSE) Schedule into personal calendars.

Deadliner does not transmit personal data to third parties. All network communication occurs directly between your local client machine and the official APIs of Moodle, Google, and schedule.kse.ua.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

When you run Deadliner, it accesses only the data strictly necessary to display or sync your assignments and classes:

  • Coursework & Deadlines: Assignment titles, due dates, course names, and submission URLs from Moodle and Google Classroom.
  • Class Schedule: Discipline titles, lecture/practice designations, scheduled time slots, and room numbers from schedule.kse.ua.
  • Calendar Events: Events created by Deadliner in your designated Google Calendar to add, update, or remove synchronized entries.

3. Google API Scopes & Limited Use Disclosure

Deadliner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deadliner requests the following OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: Used solely to create, update, and delete color-coded events (Tomato Red for deadlines, Sage Green for lectures, Peacock Blue for practices) on your personal Google Calendar. Deadliner never accesses or modifies events it did not create.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.courses.readonly: Used solely to list the courses in which you are enrolled.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.coursework.me.readonly & https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.coursework.students.readonly: Used solely to retrieve coursework deadlines assigned to your student account.

4. Local Storage & Security

Authentication tokens (Google OAuth refresh tokens, Moodle tokens, and KSE schedule tokens) are written strictly to your local machine under your user home directory:

  • ~/.deadliner.json — General configuration and platform tokens.
  • ~/.deadliner_google_token.json — Google OAuth access and refresh tokens.

These files are restricted to standard local filesystem permissions. We strongly advise users never to commit these files to public repositories.

5. Zero Third-Party Sharing & Telemetry

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data.
  • We do not include third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or analytics cookies in our CLI or on this website.
  • We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

6. Data Retention and How to Revoke Access

Because Deadliner stores no data remotely, revoking access is instantaneous and completely under your control:

  • Local Data Deletion: Remove the config files from your terminal: 
    rm ~/.deadliner.json ~/.deadliner_google_token.json
  • Google OAuth Revocation: Revoke authorization at any time through your Google Account Security Dashboard at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Calendar Event Removal: Disabling sync or deleting Deadliner events from Google Calendar permanently deletes them with no residual copies stored anywhere.

7. Contact & Maintainer Information

Deadliner is maintained as an open-source project by Vladyslav Katsel and the Deadliner engineering team at Kyiv School of Economics (KSE).

For inquiries, bug reports, or privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/vkatsel/deadliner/issues.
